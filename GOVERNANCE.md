# Governance

Vastloom uses a maintainer-led governance model during its alpha phase. Michael Gasperini
is the founding maintainer and release authority. Contributors earn broader responsibility
through sustained, technically sound work and respectful community participation.

Routine decisions are made through issues and pull-request review. Material changes to the
model format, safety envelope, architecture, compatibility policy or benchmark methodology
should begin as a public design issue and be captured in documentation when accepted.

The maintainer may reject or pause work that weakens host safety, model integrity, result
reproducibility, platform portability or legal compliance—even when it improves throughput.
Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and may be handled privately until fixed.

Releases require a clean source tree, passing declared gates, updated changelog and version,
install/package smoke tests, provenance for bundled artifacts and an honest validation report.
No contributor is entitled to project funds, trademarks, credentials or release access by
virtue of contribution alone.
